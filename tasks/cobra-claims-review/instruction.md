Ridgeline Benefit Administrators runs COBRA continuation coverage for the Northgate Industries Group Health Plan, and its claims team has a queue of medical claims from former plan members and their families. For every claim in the queue, the team needs to know whether the person had COBRA continuation coverage on the date of service. The governing rules are those of 26 CFR 54.4980B-1 through 54.4980B-10 as in force on October 1, 2026. No state continuation law or other rule applies, and the plan offers nothing beyond what those rules require. Where the rules leave a choice to the plan and nothing below says otherwise, the plan uses the shortest periods and deadlines, the highest premiums and the earliest end of coverage that the rules allow.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/plan.json describes the plan, including the applicable premium for single coverage under each coverage option for each calendar year, in cents. The file /app/data/people.json lists every person in each family who was covered under the plan on the day before the family's first qualifying event, with their relationship to the employee, date of birth, the date Medicare entitlement begins, the date coverage under another group health plan begins, and any Social Security disability determination (the date the person has been disabled since, the date of the determination and the date the person sent the plan a copy). The file /app/data/events.json lists each family's first qualifying event, all of them since mid 2022, and every later event in the family's coverage history: terminations of employment, reductions of hours, deaths of employees, divorces, children's 26th birthdays and employees' Medicare entitlement. The earliest event of each family is its first qualifying event. The file /app/data/elections.json has a record for every person the plan offered COBRA: the date the election notice was provided, the date the person sent an election (null if the person never elected), the coverage option chosen and the date the person sent the plan notice of a later event. The file /app/data/premium_payments.json lists every premium payment by person and coverage month, with the amount and the date it was sent, and, where the plan sent a notice that a payment was short, the date of that notice and the date the person sent the rest. The file /app/data/claims.json lists the claims, each with a person and a date of service.

Northgate employs well over 20 employees on a typical business day and maintains the plan throughout. The plan year is the calendar year. Under the plan, coverage ends on the last day of the month in which a qualifying event happens. COBRA coverage begins the next day, that day is the date of the loss of coverage, and the plan measures the maximum coverage period and the first 60 days of COBRA coverage from that day. A period of a number of months ends on the day before the same date that number of months later, so 18 months from April 1, 2024 end on September 30, 2025. A deadline of a number of days after a date includes the last day of the deadline. Elections, notices and payments count on the date they were sent, and election notices count on the date they were provided. A child stops being eligible under the plan on the 26th birthday. A divorce ends the spouse's eligibility but not a child's. An employee's Medicare entitlement never ends the eligibility of the employee's spouse or children. Every first qualifying event was reported to the plan on time, and every election notice explained the notices a person must send about a disability determination or a later event. Every person who elected chose single coverage under one option and pays for it. Each premium is a percentage of the applicable premium for the calendar year of the coverage month, rounded to the nearest cent with half a cent rounded up. An amount sent for one month is never applied to another month, and the plan never reinstates coverage that has ended. Medicare entitlement and other group health coverage always begin on the first day of a month, and other group health plans have no preexisting condition exclusions. When COBRA coverage ends because other coverage or Medicare begins, its last day is the day before. No one has been found no longer disabled, and no coverage ended for cause. Every date of service is on or after the day the person's COBRA coverage would begin and on or before September 30, 2026, and no claim is for a date after the person's death. The records are complete and correct as of October 15, 2026.
