Where those rules leave a choice to the plan and nothing below says otherwise, the plan gives the least coverage they allow, with the shortest periods and deadlines and the highest premiums.

The file /app/data/elections.json has a record for every person offered COBRA: the date the election notice was provided, the dates the person sent a waiver and an election (null if none), the person's coverage option and the date the person sent notice of a later event. The file /app/data/premium_payments.json lists every premium payment by person and coverage month, with the amount, the date sent and, where the plan sent notice that a payment was short, the dates of that notice and of the rest of the payment. The file /app/data/claims.json lists the claims, each with a person, a date of service and the allowed amount in cents.
