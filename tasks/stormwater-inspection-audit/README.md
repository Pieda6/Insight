# stormwater-inspection-audit

Audit of 39 construction sites in Puerto Rico under EPA's 2022 Construction General Permit (as modified April 8, 2025)
for January 1 to September 30, 2026: every inspection, report, maintenance, corrective action, track-out and
stabilization requirement of Parts 2, 4 and 5 that was missed or met late.

## Difficulty

Civil engineers and erosion and sediment control inspectors audit construction sites against the Construction General Permit, and every answer here comes from day-level records. A business day under Appendix A is a day on which construction activities take place, not a weekday, and it sets the deadlines for routine maintenance, corrective actions, track-out removal, the start of stabilization and storm inspections whose 24 hours fall outside working hours. Inspection schedules differ by area: every 7 days, or every 14 days with storm inspections under Part 4.2.2, or every 7 days with storm inspections for areas draining to sediment- or nutrient-impaired or Tier 2, 2.5 or 3 waters under Part 4.3.1, and the reduced frequency of Part 4.4.1 for stabilized areas, which ends when work resumes. Storms need an inspection after the first and the last day with 0.25 inches or more. Repeat fixes to the same control at the same location turn into corrective actions under Parts 2.1.4c and 5.1.1, which changes their deadline and adds corrective action log and SWPPP deadlines under Parts 5.2.1 and 5.4.1. Stabilization under Part 2.2.14 must start by the end of the next business day after work stops for 14 or more days or for good and finish within 14 days, or 7 days for sensitive waters or for a project that disturbed more than five acres at one time, which depends on a running acreage ledger over the whole project, so a late reopening can change an earlier deadline. The 39 sites are dense with boundaries: 0.24 and 0.25 inches of rain, inspections on day 7 and day 8, 13 and 14 idle days, exactly 5.00 and 5.10 acres, deadlines met at the exact minute or one minute late, and third and fourth repeats of the same fix. Each shortcut gets most rows right and a planted set wrong. The data is synthetic but follows real construction stormwater records.

## Solution

The reference solution builds each site's business days from the construction log and walks each area's calendar day by day, keeping its inspection schedule (Parts 4.2, 4.3.1 and 4.4.1, with the stabilized-area reduction and its end when work resumes), the next routine deadline counted from the latest inspection, and the disturbed acreage. It derives storm inspections from runs of wet days on the gauge (Part 4.2.2 and footnotes 67 to 69), report deadlines (Part 4.7.1), maintenance and corrective action deadlines with the repeat count per control, location and fix (Parts 2.1.4, 5.1 and 5.2.1), corrective action log and SWPPP deadlines (Parts 5.2.1 and 5.4.1), track-out removal (Part 2.2.4) and stabilization start and completion (Part 2.2.14 and footnotes 38 to 41), then matches each requirement to the record that met it and keeps the missed and late ones.

## Verification

The verifier recomputes every requirement with its own independently written engine (engine.py, which works in ordinal days and whole minutes and sweeps each area's schedule segments and inspections as merged events, while the reference solution walks the calendar day by day) from a private copy of the inputs that exists only in the verifier image, and never runs agent code. The data generator (authoring/generate.py) is a third implementation; all three agree on every row, and the generator rejected or repaired any random case whose answer would depend on an open reading. The engine is checked against 11 planted sites worked by hand (golden.json). Tests check that the inputs are consistent, the value formats, the sort order, the hand-worked sites, one test per rule family (business days, routine and reduced-frequency inspections, storm inspections and sensitive waters, reports and track-out, maintenance and corrective actions, stabilization and disturbed acreage) and finally the whole file by exact match. Reward is 1 only if every test passes.
