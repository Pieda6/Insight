The radiation oncology physics group is doing the independent monitor unit check for every beam of 33 treatment plans before the first fraction. For every beam, the group needs the monitor units that deliver the planned dose to the beam's calculation point. The governing method is that of the report of AAPM Task Group 71, Monitor unit calculations for photon and electron beams (Gibbons et al., Med Phys 2014;41(3):031501). No other rule or practice applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/machine.json describes the 6 MV photon beam and the 9 MeV electron beam of the department's Varian Clinac 21EX: the dose per monitor unit under normalization conditions, the collimation and accessories, and the commissioning tables, which are the CSV files beside it. The file /app/data/plans.json lists the patients and their beams. For a photon beam it gives the jaw settings, the field shaping, the equivalent square of the shaped field, the wedge, the source to surface distance on the central axis, the calculation point, the tissue layers along the central axis with their electron density relative to water and the heterogeneity correction to use, and the dose in cGy the beam must deliver to the point. For an electron beam it gives the applicator, the insert, the source to surface distance, the isodose line the dose is prescribed to, the method for an extended distance and the dose in cGy. The file /app/data/tg71.txt holds the text of the report as the department keeps it.

Each beam enters a flat surface perpendicular to its central axis. Jaw settings and the equivalent square of a shaped field are at the isocenter, 100 cm from the source; the equivalent square of a shaped field is the one the planning system reports for the field incident on the patient. The depth of a calculation point is measured along the direction of the central axis, and its off-axis position is measured in the plane through the point perpendicular to the central axis. An electron insert size is the size of the field projected to the isocenter, and every electron insert has the depth of maximum dose given in machine.json. Tables are interpolated linearly. The records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/monitor_units.csv. The first line must be exactly beam_id,mu and it must be followed by one row for each beam, in the order of /app/data/plans.json. The mu column is the monitor units as a decimal number with at least two decimal places. Each value is accepted within 0.3 percent, so do not round to whole monitor units, and do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.

You have 28800 seconds to complete this task. Do not cheat by using online solutions or hints specific to this task.
