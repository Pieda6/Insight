The intensive care unit's quality team is auditing acute kidney injury in 120 adult admissions. For every admission, the team needs the highest stage of acute kidney injury the patient reached, the time the patient first met a criterion for acute kidney injury, and the time the patient first reached that highest stage. The governing rules are those of the KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for Acute Kidney Injury (Kidney Int Suppl 2012;2:1-138) for defining and staging acute kidney injury. No other rule or practice applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/patients.json gives each admission's start and end, the patient's age, the weight in kg at admission, and the patient's known serum creatinine before admission in mg/dl. The file /app/data/creatinine.csv holds every serum creatinine result as the laboratory reported it, with the time the blood was drawn, in mg/dL or umol/L (the laboratory converts at 88.4 umol/L per mg/dL); a corrected result replaces the earlier result with the same accession, and a specimen that was rejected or cancelled has no result. The file /app/data/urine.csv holds every charted urine volume in ml, each the volume collected since the previous charting, the first since the admission started. The file /app/data/rrt.csv gives the time renal replacement therapy started, for the admissions where it did. Times are local New York time (America/New_York), to the minute.

Every admission lasts at most 7 days, and every rise of serum creatinine above the known value before admission happened during the admission. A result counts from the time the blood was drawn. Urine flowed evenly over each charting interval, and output per kg uses the weight at admission. A urine output criterion is met at the first moment that the urine collected over the stated number of hours just before it meets the criterion, within the charted record. The records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/aki_staging.csv. The first line must be exactly patient_id,max_stage,aki_onset,stage_reached and it must be followed by one row for each admission, in the order of /app/data/patients.json. The max_stage column is the highest stage, 0 to 3, with 0 when the patient never met a criterion; aki_onset and stage_reached are local times in the form 2026-03-08 14:05, rounded to the nearest minute, or none when max_stage is 0. Stages are compared exactly and times are accepted within 1 minute, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
