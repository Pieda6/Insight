The awards team at a VA regional office is auditing the disability compensation it pays to a group of veterans. For every veteran, the team needs to know, for each month from January 2023 through September 2026, the combined disability rating on which that month's compensation is based and the amount of compensation VA pays for the month. The governing rules are those of 38 CFR Part 3 and Part 4 as in force on October 1, 2026. No other law or rule applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/veterans.json gives each veteran's last day of active service and any separation pay the veteran received, with its gross amount and the federal income tax withheld from it, in cents. The file /app/data/disabilities.json lists each veteran's service-connected disabilities, with a description and the diagnostic code under which each is rated. The file /app/data/rating_decisions.json lists every rating decision on those disabilities: an original claim for service connection or a claim for an increased rating, with the date VA received the claim, the date the evidence first shows the disability or the increase in its severity and the rating assigned, or a reduction, with the date VA sent the veteran notice of the final rating action and the reduced rating. The file /app/data/hospital_and_convalescence.json lists hospital treatment, surgery and cast immobilization for service-connected disabilities: the date of admission or treatment, the date treatment of the service-connected disability began, the date of discharge or release, every authorized absence during a hospital stay (its first and last day away), and the months of convalescence VA granted. The file /app/data/dependents.json lists each veteran's spouse and children, with the date of the marriage or birth and the date the veteran reported the dependent to VA. The file /app/data/compensation_rates.json gives the monthly compensation rates, in cents, that apply from each effective date, by rating and by whether the veteran has a spouse, a spouse and one child, or one child, with the amount added for each further child.

Every decision granted the rating shown for the disability shown, every claim was complete when VA received it, and every report of a dependent came with the evidence VA needed. Before every reduction VA sent the veteran the proposed reduction, and the veteran sent no evidence and asked for no hearing. Every hospital is a VA or approved hospital, every authorized absence listed is an absence and not a release approved as part of the treatment plan, VA found that every surgery or cast listed meets 38 CFR 4.30(a), and every month of convalescence granted was required. No veteran has a total rating based on individual unemployability, special monthly compensation or a rating under 38 CFR 3.324. The veterans are alive, receive no military retired pay, drill pay or other VA benefit, and were never incarcerated. Every marriage continues, every child is the veteran's own child and is under 18 throughout, and no spouse needs aid and attendance. All separation pay is nondisability separation pay for the same period of service in which the disabilities were incurred. VA pays every amount it owes and nothing more, and nothing else is withheld.

Write your results to /app/output/monthly_payments.csv. The first line must be exactly veteran_id,month,combined_rating,payment and it must be followed by one row for each veteran and month, with the veterans in the order of /app/data/veterans.json and, for each veteran, the months from 2023-01 to 2026-09 in order. The month column has the form 2024-07. The combined_rating column is the combined disability rating on which VA pays compensation for the month, as a whole number such as 70 (100 for a month paid under a temporary total rating), and 0 for a month for which no compensable rating is payable. The payment column is the compensation VA pays for the month, in dollars with two decimal places, as in 1234.56, and 0.00 when it pays nothing. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
