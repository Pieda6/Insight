The awards team at a VA regional office is auditing the disability compensation it pays to a group of veterans. For every veteran, the team needs to know, for each month from January 2023 through September 2026, the veteran's combined disability rating at the end of the month and the amount of compensation VA pays for that month. The governing rules are those of 38 CFR Part 3 and Part 4 as in force on October 1, 2026. No other law or rule applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/veterans.json gives each veteran's last day of active service and any separation pay the veteran received, with its gross amount and the federal income tax withheld from it, in cents. The file /app/data/disabilities.json lists each veteran's service-connected disabilities, with a description and the diagnostic code under which it is rated. The file /app/data/rating_decisions.json lists every rating decision on those disabilities: whether it decided an original claim for service connection or a claim for an increased rating, the date VA received the claim, the date the evidence first shows the disability or the increase in its severity, and the rating assigned. The file /app/data/dependents.json lists each veteran's spouse and children, with the date of the marriage or birth and the date the veteran reported the dependent to VA. The file /app/data/compensation_rates.json gives the monthly compensation rates, in cents, that apply from each effective date, by rating and by whether the veteran has a spouse, a spouse and one child, or one child, with the amount added for each further child.

Every decision granted the rating shown for the disability shown, every claim was complete when VA received it, and every report of a dependent came with the evidence VA needed. No rating was ever reduced or severed, and no veteran had a temporary total rating, a total rating based on individual unemployability, special monthly compensation or a rating under 38 CFR 3.324. The veterans are alive, receive no military retired pay, drill pay or other VA benefit, and were never incarcerated. Every marriage continues, every child is the veteran's own child and is under 18 throughout, and no spouse needs aid and attendance. All separation pay is nondisability separation pay for the same period of service in which the disabilities were incurred. VA pays every amount it owes and nothing more, and nothing else is withheld.

Write your results to /app/output/monthly_payments.csv. The first line must be exactly veteran_id,month,combined_rating,payment and it must be followed by one row for each veteran and month, with the veterans in the order of /app/data/veterans.json and, for each veteran, the months from 2023-01 to 2026-09 in order. The month column has the form 2024-07. The combined_rating column is the veteran's combined disability rating on the last day of the month, as a whole number such as 70, and 0 when the veteran has no service-connected disability in effect. The payment column is the compensation VA pays for the month, in dollars with two decimal places, as in 1234.56, and 0.00 when it pays nothing. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
