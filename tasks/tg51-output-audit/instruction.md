A regional radiation oncology physics network independently reviews the annual output calibrations of its member clinics. Twelve clinics have sent their worksheets for every photon and electron beam they calibrated this year, and for every beam the network needs the absorbed dose to water per monitor unit at the reference point and at the depth of maximum dose. The governing method is that of the AAPM TG-51 protocol for clinical reference dosimetry of high-energy photon and electron beams (Almond et al., Med Phys 1999;26(9):1847-1870) together with its addendum for photon beams (McEwen et al., Med Phys 2014;41(4):041501). No other rule or practice applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/audit.json lists each clinic with its electrometer (the correction factor for each range, from its calibration certificate), its ion chambers and its beams. A chamber entry gives the model, the cavity radius and, for a cylindrical chamber, the length of its active volume. A chamber calibrated at an accredited laboratory has its 60Co absorbed-dose-to-water calibration coefficient with the unit, and the polarity and voltage of that calibration; a plane-parallel chamber calibrated against a cylindrical chamber instead names the beam and the chamber of that cross-calibration. A beam entry gives the machine, the energy, whether the beam is flattening-filter-free, whether the dose was measured in an SSD or an SAD set-up (with the clinic's TMR at 10 cm for an SAD set-up), the chamber used for the dose measurement and the chamber used for each depth scan. The file /app/data/readings.csv holds every chamber reading in nC as the electrometer displayed it, with its monitor units, polarity, voltage, electrometer range, the water temperature near the chamber and the barometric pressure, for the dose measurement (reference) and for the two halves of a cross-calibration; a row whose purpose ends in _gradient was taken with the chamber's centre half a cavity radius deeper. The file /app/data/depth_scans.csv holds the raw central-axis scans taken at SSD 100 cm with a 10 x 10 cm field, by the depth of the centre of a cylindrical chamber or of the front of the air cavity of a plane-parallel chamber, in the open beam and with a 1 mm lead foil 50 cm or 30 cm above the water surface. The file /app/data/profiles.csv gives, for each photon beam, the relative dose along the long axis of the reference chamber at the reference depth, by distance from the central axis. The file /app/data/electron_pdd.csv gives each electron beam's clinical percentage depth dose. The files addendum_kq.csv, tg51_photon_kq.csv and tg51_kecal.csv are Table I of the addendum and Tables I, II and III of TG-51.

Photon doses were measured with the point of measurement at 10 cm depth, and electron doses at the reference depth of TG-51 for the beam. Leakage was below 0.1 percent of every reading. Pressures are the clinic barometer's readings, not corrected to sea level. Tables and curves are interpolated linearly. The records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/dose_per_mu.csv. The first line must be exactly beam_id,dose_ref_cgy_per_mu,dose_dmax_cgy_per_mu and it must be followed by one row for each beam, in the order of /app/data/audit.json. The column dose_ref_cgy_per_mu is the absorbed dose to water per monitor unit at the reference point in the set-up of the dose measurement, and dose_dmax_cgy_per_mu is the absorbed dose to water per monitor unit at the depth of maximum dose on the central axis in the same set-up, both in cGy per MU. Each value is accepted within 0.3 percent of the correct value. Do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
