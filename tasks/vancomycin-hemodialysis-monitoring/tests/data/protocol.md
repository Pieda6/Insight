# Vancomycin Dosing in Maintenance Hemodialysis

Dialysis Unit Policy DU-VAN-04, effective January 1, 2026. Applies to every patient on maintenance hemodialysis who
receives vancomycin for an MRSA infection. Where this policy is silent, the 2020 consensus guideline of ASHP, IDSA,
PIDS and SIDP (Rybak et al., Am J Health-Syst Pharm 2020;77:835-864) governs.

## 1. Weight-based doses

1.1 Loading and maintenance doses in mg/kg are those of the guideline's Recommendation 13 for the dialyzer
permeability and the dose timing of the session. Where the guideline gives a range, the unit uses its midpoint.

1.2 A weight-based dose is the dose in mg/kg multiplied by the patient's weight before that session.

1.3 The patient's first session under this policy receives the loading dose. A loading dose does not exceed the
maximum the guideline sets for loading doses.

## 2. Predialysis levels

2.1 The predialysis level for a session is the latest numeric result drawn no more than 2 hours before the session
starts, among the results the guideline's advice on blood sampling in patients on hemodialysis allows.

2.2 The target range for a predialysis level is 15 to 20 mg/L, as Recommendation 14 advises.

## 3. Maintenance dose

3.1 The current maintenance dose starts at the weight-based maintenance dose of the patient's second session.

3.2 At a session whose dialyzer permeability or dose timing differs from that of the session before, the current
maintenance dose restarts at that session's weight-based maintenance dose, and that session receives it.

3.3 Otherwise, when the session's predialysis level is outside the target range, the current maintenance dose is
changed in proportion to bring the level to 17.5 mg/L, and that session receives the changed dose. When the level
is above 25 mg/L, the session's dose is held and the changed dose applies from the next session.

3.4 Otherwise the session receives the current maintenance dose.

## 4. Rounding

4.1 Each dose is rounded to the nearest 250 mg when it is set, and the rounded dose is carried forward. No other
value is rounded.
