The dialysis unit's pharmacy is auditing the vancomycin doses for 150 patients on maintenance hemodialysis who are being treated for MRSA infections. For every dialysis session, the pharmacy needs the predialysis vancomycin level that counts, how that level compares with the guideline's predialysis target, and the dose the patient should receive at that session. The governing rules are those of the 2020 consensus guideline of ASHP, IDSA, PIDS and SIDP on vancomycin therapeutic monitoring (Rybak et al., Am J Health-Syst Pharm 2020;77:835-864) for patients receiving intermittent hemodialysis. No other rule or practice applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/sessions.csv lists every dialysis session with its start and stop, the dialyzer's permeability to vancomycin (high or low), whether that session's vancomycin dose is given during the session (intradialytic) or after it ends (after_dialysis), and the patient's weight in kilograms before the session. The file /app/data/levels.csv holds every vancomycin result as the laboratory reported it, with the time the blood was drawn, in mg/L or umol/L (vancomycin, 1449.3 g/mol); a corrected result replaces the earlier result with the same accession, and a specimen that was rejected or cancelled has no result. Times are local New York time (America/New_York), to the minute.

Every patient started vancomycin at their first session in the data and receives a dose at every session. Every session listed took place as recorded, no other session took place, and the records are complete and correct. The unit draws predialysis blood in the 2 hours before a session starts, and when more than one result could serve as a session's predialysis level it uses the latest. Where the guideline gives a range of doses, the unit uses its midpoint, and weight-based doses use the weight before that session. When a predialysis level is outside the target, the unit changes the dose it would otherwise give in proportion, to bring the level to 17.5 mg/L. The pharmacy prepares every dose rounded to the nearest 250 mg, and that is the dose given.

Write your results to /app/output/session_doses.csv. The first line must be exactly patient_id,session_id,predialysis_level_mg_l,status,dose_mg and it must be followed by one row for each session, sorted by patient_id and then by session start. The predialysis_level_mg_l column is the session's predialysis level in mg/L with one decimal, or empty when there is none; status is none, below, within or above for that level against the target; and dose_mg is the dose in mg for that session. Levels are accepted within 0.05 mg/L, and status and dose_mg are compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
