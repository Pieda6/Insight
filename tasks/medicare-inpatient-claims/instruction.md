Lakeview Health runs general hospitals, critical access hospitals, psychiatric hospitals and skilled nursing facilities, and its patient financial services team has a queue of Medicare Part A inpatient claims to settle. For every claim, the team needs to know which benefit period the stay falls in, how many days of the stay Part A pays for and how many of those are lifetime reserve days, and what the beneficiary owes as inpatient hospital deductible, daily coinsurance and blood deductible, so that the balance can be billed to the beneficiary or a supplemental insurer. The governing rules are those of Medicare Part A in 42 CFR Part 409 as in force on October 1, 2026, together with the deductible and coinsurance amounts that CMS published for each calendar year. No other rule, manual or policy applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/beneficiaries.json lists each beneficiary with the first day of their Part A entitlement. The file /app/data/facilities.json gives each facility's type. The file /app/data/stays.json holds every inpatient stay each beneficiary has had since becoming entitled, together with any stay that was already under way on the first day of entitlement. Each stay gives the facility, the inpatient admission date and the discharge date. If the patient was in outpatient observation before being admitted, it also gives the date the observation began. For a general or critical access hospital stay, it says whether the patient was treated for mental illness. For a skilled nursing facility stay, it gives the last day on which the patient's care met the skilled level of care requirements of § 409.31(b)(1) and (3); after that day the patient received custodial care only. Every stay lists the blood furnished during it, with the product, the number of units, the facility's charge per unit, and whether the blood was replaced or obtained by the facility at no charge other than a processing charge. The file /app/data/claims.json is the queue, with one claim for each stay admitted on or after January 1, 2025. The file /app/data/part_b_blood.json lists blood the beneficiaries received as outpatients under Part B. The file /app/data/part_a_amounts.json gives the published amounts for 2025 and 2026 in cents.

Every beneficiary has been entitled to Part A on the basis of age without interruption since the date given and has never been enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan. Lakeview is the only provider any of them has used, so the records are their complete Part A history. Every facility has participated in Medicare since 2001. Every hospital, including each psychiatric hospital, meets the requirements of section 1861(e)(1) of the Social Security Act, each critical access hospital meets section 1820 and each skilled nursing facility meets sections 1819(a)(1) and 1861(y). Every inpatient stay was medically necessary. No beneficiary has filed an election not to use lifetime reserve days, and every facility's charges for a stay or a day of care are higher than any deductible or coinsurance amount that could apply to them.

A beneficiary is an inpatient on the day of admission and on every following day up to, but not including, the day of discharge, and those are the days of care in the stay. Time in outpatient observation before the inpatient admission date is outpatient time. The inpatient hospital deductible for a benefit period is charged on the claim for the stay in which Part A first pays for hospital or critical access hospital care in that period. Every stay in the queue falls within a benefit period. Dates are calendar dates, and the records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/claim_determinations.csv. The first line must be exactly claim_id,benefit_period_start,covered_days,reserve_days,deductible,coinsurance,blood and it must be followed by one row for each claim, in the order of /app/data/claims.json. The benefit_period_start column gives the first day of the benefit period during which the stay took place, in the form 2025-03-02. The covered_days column gives the number of days of the stay that Part A pays for, and reserve_days gives how many of those are lifetime reserve days. The deductible column gives the inpatient hospital deductible charged on the claim, coinsurance gives the total daily coinsurance for the claim, and blood gives the blood deductible the beneficiary owes for blood furnished during the stay. Write these three amounts in dollars with two decimal places, as in 1676.00, and write 0.00 when nothing is owed. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
