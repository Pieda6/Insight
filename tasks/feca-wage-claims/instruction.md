The injury compensation office of a federal agency is auditing the continuation of pay and wage-loss compensation on its employees' work injury claims. For every claim, the office needs the number of days charged to continuation of pay, the last day charged, and the number of days and the total amount of wage-loss compensation payable through September 30, 2026. The governing rules are those of the Federal Employees' Compensation Act (5 U.S.C. 8101 and following) and 20 CFR Part 10 as in force on October 1, 2026. No other law, rule or agency policy applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/employees.json describes each employee: the pay plan (General Schedule or wage grade), whether the employee works full time or part time, the hours scheduled on each day of the week, the time the scheduled day begins, for wage grade employees the days in the workweek, the history of pay rates in cents with the date each took effect (for General Schedule employees the annual rate of basic pay and any premium pay under 5 U.S.C. 5545(c)(1) as a percentage of it, for wage grade employees the daily wage), overtime pay in the year before the injury, and the employee's spouse and children. The file /app/data/claims.json lists the claims, each with its type (a traumatic injury on Form CA-1 or an occupational disease on Form CA-2), the local date and time of the injury, and the date the employee filed the notice with the supervisor. The file /app/data/time_and_attendance.json lists every absence from work after the injury, with its first and last day (time lost on the day of injury after the employee had started work that day is not listed) and how the employee asked to be paid for it; where hours are shown, the absence was that many hours on that one day. The file /app/data/medical_evidence.json lists the periods in which the medical evidence shows each employee totally disabled. The file /app/data/cost_of_living_adjustments.json lists the cost-of-living adjustments under 5 U.S.C. 8146a.

No employee works for the Postal Service, every employee is a United States citizen, and every injury and disease happened in the performance of duty without misconduct. OWCP accepted every claim, found every absence listed and every period in the medical evidence to be due to the accepted injury or disease, and accepted every new period of absence after a return to work as a recurrence, for which the employee filed Form CA-2a and elected regular pay. An absence covers the scheduled hours of every scheduled workday from its first to its last day, and an absence of a few hours on one day was for medical examination or treatment of the injury. Every scheduled workday that no absence covers was worked in full in the regular full-time or part-time job held at the time of injury, and no one worked on an unscheduled day. Where continuation of pay is not payable for an absence the employee asked to have paid as continuation of pay, that absence is leave without pay. Medical evidence always reached the employer on time, no one was offered, worked or refused another position, no appointment or employment ended, and no disability is permanent. Every employee worked in the employment held at the time of injury for the whole year before it; General Schedule employees have a fixed annual rate of pay and wage grade employees do not. OWCP did not use the actual daily wage under 5 U.S.C. 8114(c). It pays compensation on a weekly basis, one seventh of the weekly amount for each calendar day, keeps every amount exact and rounds only the total for a claim to the nearest cent. No compensation reaches the maximum or the minimum of 20 CFR 10.406. Spouses and children live with the employee, every child is unmarried, school enrollment shown is full-time attendance as a student, and no other relative is a dependent. A period of a number of days or months after a date ends on the date that many days or months later, so 30 days after March 4 end on April 3. The records are complete and correct as of October 15, 2026.

Write your results to /app/output/claim_determinations.csv. The first line must be exactly claim_id,cop_days,last_cop_day,compensation_days,compensation and it must be followed by one row for each claim, in the order of /app/data/claims.json. The cop_days column is the number of days charged toward the 45 days of continuation of pay, last_cop_day is the last day charged in the form 2025-03-21 (none when no day is charged), compensation_days is the number of calendar days of wage-loss compensation payable through September 30, 2026, and compensation is the total wage-loss compensation payable for those days in dollars with two decimal places, as in 1234.56, or 0.00. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
