Riverbend Auto Group is holding a Fall Sales Event in October 2026, and its marketing team has to clear every financing advertisement for the event before any of them run. You are reviewing those ads for the team. The only law that governs this review is Regulation Z, 12 CFR Part 1026, together with its official interpretations in Supplement I, as that rule stood on October 1, 2026. No other federal or state law, industry guideline or house policy applies, so wherever this description leaves a question to the regulation, the regulation decides it.

The materials are in /app/data/. The file /app/data/deal_worksheets.json holds one worksheet for each offer the dealer group is advertising. A worksheet records the cash price of the vehicle, the down payment, the contract rate, whether that rate may increase after consummation, the contract date, the date of the first payment, and the monthly payment schedule, given as the number of payments and the amount of each one. It also itemizes the charges that come with the deal, and for each charge it records a set of facts about that charge. The file /app/data/ads.csv has one row per advertisement, with the columns ad_id, offer_id, medium and the full ad copy, where medium is one of print, web_banner, direct_mail, radio or tv. The file /app/data/dealer_group.json describes the stores in the group, and each store is the creditor on the retail installment sale contracts it writes. Every money amount anywhere in the data is expressed in cents.

Some questions the regulation leaves open have already been settled for this review, and you should treat the answers as fixed. The worksheet an ad points to through its offer_id is the credit transaction that ad advertises. Every loan is closed-end consumer credit, and none is secured by a dwelling. Every charge listed on a worksheet is financed as part of the contract, while the down payment is paid in cash. The facts recorded on each worksheet are complete and true, so each charge should be judged only on the facts given for it, without assuming anything that is not there. The copy in /app/data/ads.csv is the whole advertisement, with nothing left out, and everything in it counts as clear and conspicuous. Where a worksheet gives a coverage term of "full contract term", that coverage runs for the whole term of the transaction. A phone number is toll-free if its area code is 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844 or 833. No other number in the data is toll-free, and none of them lets callers reverse the charges.

Your results go in /app/output/ad_clearance.csv, and you may create the /app/output/ directory if it is not already there. The first line of the file must be exactly ad_id,finance_charge,amount_financed,apr,apr_accurate,missing_disclosures,cleared and after it comes one row for each ad, in the same order the ads appear in /app/data/ads.csv. The finance_charge and amount_financed columns give the finance charge and the amount financed for the offer the ad advertises, in dollars with exactly two decimal places, in the form 1234.56, and both are checked for an exact match. The apr column gives that offer's annual percentage rate as a percentage rounded to two decimal places, in the form 4.37, and it is accepted if it is within 0.01 of the expected figure.

The apr_accurate column is true when the rate the ad states is accurate for that offer, false when it is not, and n/a when the ad states no rate at all. The missing_disclosures column names each disclosure the ad needed to make but does not, using only four names: apr for the rate stated as an annual percentage rate, downpayment for the amount or percentage of the down payment, repayment_terms for the terms of repayment, and rate_increase for the fact that the rate may increase after consummation. Give the names in alphabetical order, separated by semicolons with no spaces, or write none when nothing is missing. The cleared column is true only when the rate the ad states, if it states one, is accurate and nothing is missing, and it is false in every other case.

Write true and false in lowercase. Do not add extra columns, quoting that changes a value, or blank lines anywhere in the file. Every row is compared against the expected result, so a missing, extra or out-of-order row counts against you just as a wrong value does. The ad copy, the worksheets and the store records are all you have to go on, and each ad should be judged on its own copy and its own offer.
