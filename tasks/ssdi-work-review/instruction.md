Granite Mutual's long-term disability claims unit offsets its benefit payments by any Social Security disability insurance benefit its claimants receive, so it has to know, month by month, whether Social Security owes each claimant a benefit. Many of these claimants have gone back to work, and the unit needs a determination for every month of their entitlement, from its first month through December 2025, so that it can settle the offsets for those years in one pass. Decide each month under the Social Security disability rules in 20 CFR Part 404 as in force on October 1, 2026, using the monthly amounts that the Social Security Administration published for each year. No other rule, program or policy applies.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/beneficiaries.json lists each claimant with the date their disability began, the date they filed their application for disability insurance benefits, and whether they are statutorily blind. The file /app/data/earnings.json lists, for each claimant and month, the gross wages earned in that month, the value of any employer subsidy in that month, and the impairment-related work expenses for that month, all in whole dollars; a month that is not listed had no earnings. The file /app/data/annual_amounts.json gives the published monthly trial work period amount and the substantial gainful activity amounts for blind and non-blind individuals for each year.

Every claimant was found disabled from the onset date given and stayed disabled throughout, with no medical improvement, and none had an earlier period of disability. A claimant shown as statutorily blind was blind within the meaning of the Social Security Act throughout. Each is insured, is entitled only to disability insurance benefits on their own record, stays below full retirement age through 2025, and filed only the one application. Every claimant worked only as an employee, wages are counted in the month in which they were earned, and the subsidy and expense amounts are the values Social Security determined for that month. None of the claimants did any work in the 12 months after the onset of disability. Each month's earnings are evaluated on their own, without averaging across months, and none of the work in the data was an unsuccessful work attempt. A month's earnings for trial work purposes are its gross wages. No claimant requested expedited reinstatement or filed a new application, and no benefit was withheld for any reason other than the ones these rules provide. The records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/monthly_determinations.csv. The first line must be exactly beneficiary_id,month,benefit_payable,trial_work_month and it must be followed by one row for each claimant and each month from the claimant's first month of entitlement to disability insurance benefits through December 2025, sorted by beneficiary_id and then by month. Write the month in the form 2023-07. The benefit_payable column is yes when a disability insurance benefit is payable for that month and no when it is not. The trial_work_month column is yes when the month is a month of services counted in the claimant's trial work period and no otherwise. Write yes and no in lowercase. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
