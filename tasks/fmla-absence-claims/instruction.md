Keystone Absence Services administers Family and Medical Leave Act leave for four client employers, and its claims examiners have a backlog of absence claims to designate. For every absence in the backlog, the examiners need to know how many of the hours the employee was away count as FMLA leave. The governing rules are those of 29 CFR Part 825 as in force on October 1, 2026. No state leave law, employer policy or other rule applies, and nothing an employer offers beyond the FMLA matters here.

All of the input data is in /app/data/. The file /app/data/employers.json describes each employer: the method it has chosen for the 12-month period in which its employees' 12 workweeks of leave are measured, the first day of its leave year when it uses a fixed leave year, its workweek, its holidays and any periods when its business was closed and employees were not expected to report for work. The file /app/data/employees.json gives each employee's employer, hire date, the hours the employee is scheduled to work on each day of the week, and whether the employee is otherwise scheduled and expected to work on holidays. The file /app/data/absences.json lists every absence each employee has had since mid 2024, with its reason, whether it was a continuous absence from a first day to a last day or an intermittent absence of a stated number of hours on a single day, and, for bonding leave, the child's date of birth.

Every employee has worked continuously for their employer since the hire date given, has worked at least 1,250 hours in every 12-month period that matters here once they have been employed for 12 months, and works at a site with at least 50 employees within 75 miles. Every absence was for the reason stated, that reason is FMLA-qualifying, and the employee gave proper notice and supplied any certification requested. Bonding leave is to bond with the employee's own newborn child. Military caregiver leave is to care for one covered servicemember with a serious injury or illness, and every employee who takes it takes it for that one servicemember only. Each employer applies its chosen method to all of its employees, and each employer designates as FMLA leave every hour that qualifies. Leave is measured in hours: a workweek of leave is the number of hours the employee is scheduled to work in a week, and a continuous absence covers all of the hours the employee was scheduled to work on each day of it. Employees work their schedules exactly, with no overtime. Under a rolling 12-month period, the 12 months before a day of leave begin on the same calendar date one year earlier, which is not included, and end on the day before. The data begins far enough back that no earlier leave affects the answers, and the records are complete and correct.

Write your results to /app/output/fmla_hours.csv. The first line must be exactly absence_id,fmla_hours and it must be followed by one row for each absence, in the order of /app/data/absences.json. The fmla_hours column gives the number of hours of that absence that are FMLA leave, with two decimal places, as in 37.50, and 0.00 when none of it is. Every row is compared exactly, so do not add extra columns, blank lines or explanations.
