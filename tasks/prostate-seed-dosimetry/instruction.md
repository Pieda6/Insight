The radiation oncology physics service is completing the post-implant evaluations of 20 patients treated with permanent prostate seed implants of iodine-125 or palladium-103. Dose is calculated under the AAPM TG-43U1 protocol (Rivard et al., Update of AAPM Task Group No. 43 Report: A revised AAPM protocol for brachytherapy dose calculations, Med Phys 2004;31:633-674), and the service's post-implant report follows AAPM TG-137 (Nath et al., AAPM recommendations on dose prescription and reporting methods for permanent interstitial brachytherapy for prostate cancer, Med Phys 2009;36:5310-5322).

The data is in /app/data/. patients.json gives each patient's implant time and CT time in New York local time (America/New_York), the prescription in Gy, and the prostate volume in cc measured by ultrasound before the implant (volume_preimplant_cc), by ultrasound at the end of the procedure (volume_postimplant_cc) and as contoured on the CT (volume_ct_cc). batches.csv gives each seed batch's model and the source strength from its vendor certificate: strength_unit is U_per_seed, U_total for the whole batch of seeds_in_batch seeds, or mCi_apparent with the exposure-rate constant the vendor used in R·cm²·mCi⁻¹·h⁻¹, and calibrated_at is the calibration time with its UTC offset. seeds.csv lists every seed found on the CT with the positions of its end markers in millimetres; a seed whose orientation could not be resolved has one marker and blank second-marker fields; review names the seed that a duplicate detection repeats. contours.json gives, for each patient, the prostate, urethra and rectum as closed polygons in the same CT coordinates on each 2.5 mm slice. source_data.json holds the report's consensus data for each seed model (Tables I to XI and XIII). tg43u1_formalism.txt is the text of the report's Section III and Appendices C, D and E.

The service's practice settles these points:
- A seed with two markers is centred at their midpoint with its axis through them and follows the 2D formalism with the line-source geometry function. A seed with one marker is centred at that marker and follows the 1D formalism of Eq. 11 with the line-source geometry function; closer than the first tabulated distance of the anisotropy factor it follows the short-distance model of Eq. C3 with the line-source geometry function.
- Between tabulated values, ln g_L(r) is linear in r, F(r, θ) is linear in r and in θ, and φan(r) is linear in r. Beyond their tabulated distances, g_L, F and φan keep the nearest tabulated value, as in Eq. C1. Above the last tabulated angle, F is interpolated to its value of 1 at 90 degrees.
- The prostate volume t days after the implant is V(t) = V_pre + (V_0 - V_pre)·exp(-λe·t), where V_pre is volume_preimplant_cc, V_0 is volume_postimplant_cc, and λe is the value for which V at the CT time equals volume_ct_cc.
- The prostate and the tissue around it swell and shrink uniformly: every distance between a seed and a tissue point is its CT value times s(t) = (V(t)/V_CT)^(1/3), and seed orientations do not change.
- The dose to a tissue point is the integral, from the implant time to complete decay, of the dose rate from all accepted seeds at the geometry of each moment, with each seed's air-kerma strength decayed from its certificate to the implant.
- Voxels are 2.5 mm cubes with centres at (i + 1/2)·2.5 mm in x and y and at the slice positions in z. A voxel belongs to a structure when its centre lies inside the structure's polygon on its slice by the even-odd rule. The urethra is not subtracted from the prostate.
- With a structure's N voxels sorted by dose in descending order, D_p is the dose at rank ceil(p·N/100). V100 and V150 are the percentages of prostate voxels receiving at least 100 and 150 percent of the prescription. Rectal V100 is the number of rectum voxels receiving at least the prescription times the voxel volume. Rectal D2cc is the dose at rank ceil(2.0 cc / voxel volume).
- Criteria: D90 at or above the prescription; V100 at or above 95.00 percent; urethral D10 at or below 150 percent of the prescription; rectal V100 at or below 1.000 cc.
- Nothing is rounded before the written values.
