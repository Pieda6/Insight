## 20 CFR 10.102(a)(1): CA-7 needed when disabled with loss of pay for more than three calendar days.
## 20 CFR 10.111(d): Postal Service employees: no compensation or COP for the waiting period (first three days); leave/LWOP;
if disability exceeds 14 days, leave reinstated or LWOP paid. Days of time loss for medical treatment only, with no
work-related disability, do not count as part of the waiting period.

## 20 CFR 10.401
(a) Compensation is payable when an employee starts to lose pay if the injury causes permanent disability or if pay loss
continues for more than 14 calendar days. Otherwise, compensation is payable on the fourth day after pay stops pursuant to
5 U.S.C. 8117(a). Compensation may not be paid while an injured employee is in a continuation of pay status or receives pay for
leave or, for Postal Service employees, for the first three days of temporary disability.
(b) Total disability: 66 2/3 percent of the pay rate with no dependents, 75 percent with at least one dependent.
## 10.403 partial disability (Shadrick): WEC% = earnings / current pay rate; WEC$ = pay rate for comp x WEC%; LWEC = pay rate - WEC$;
compensation 66 2/3 or 75 percent of LWEC.
## 10.405 dependents: spouse; unmarried child under 18; over 18 incapable of self-support; student to 23 or four years beyond
high school; wholly dependent parent.
## 10.406 (a) max: 75% of basic monthly pay of GS-15 step 10 (no locality); (b) min for total disability: 75% of basic monthly pay
of GS-2 step 1 or actual pay, whichever is less.
## 10.420 COLA (5 U.S.C. 8146a): (a) disability began more than one year before the COLA took effect; COP or leave during the
period does not affect the one-year computation. (c) recurrence pay rate: the effective date of that pay rate began more than
one year before the COLA.
## 10.421(c): no total disability compensation concurrently with severance or separation pay.
## 10.425: compensation may be claimed for restorable annual and sick leave (CA-7a, CA-7b, leave buy back).

## 5 U.S.C. 8105(a): total disability: 66 2/3 percent of monthly pay.
## 5 U.S.C. 8110: dependents (spouse in household / receiving contributions / court order; unmarried child under 18 or over 18
incapable; wholly dependent parent; student continuation); (b) augmented at 8 1/3 percent of monthly pay.
## 5 U.S.C. 8114: (c) monthly pay = 1/12 of average annual earnings; weekly = 1/52. For up to 90 calendar days from the beginning
of compensable disability, compensation may be computed on the actual daily wage, paid for the days he would have worked.
(d)(1) worked substantially the whole year: (A) fixed annual rate -> that rate; (B) not fixed -> daily wage (or average) x 300
(6-day week), 280 (5 1/2-day), 260 (5-day). (2) not the whole year but the position would afford a whole year: same-class
employee's earnings. (3) otherwise reasonable; not less than 150 x average daily wage. (e) includes subsistence, quarters,
in-kind, premium pay under 5545(c)(1); excludes overtime pay, cost-of-living differentials abroad, bonus/premium for
extraordinary service.
## 5 U.S.C. 8117(a): no compensation for the first 3 days of temporary disability except when disability exceeds 14 days,
is followed by permanent disability, or under 8103/8104. (b) Postal: no compensation or COP for the first 3 days; leave or LWOP;
if over 14 days or permanent, leave reinstated / LWOP paid.
## 5 U.S.C. 8118: (a) COP for a claim for wage loss due to a traumatic injury filed with the immediate superior within the time in
8122(a)(2) [30 days]. (b) furnished without a break in time, except under 8117(b), unless controverted; not to exceed 45 days.
(c) the employee may use annual or sick leave at the time disability begins, but compensation does not begin, and the 8117
periods do not begin to run, until COP ends or the use of leave ends. (e) COP is not compensation.

## 20 CFR Part 10 Subpart C (continuation of pay), pasted by the author
10.200 (a) employer continues regular pay during periods of disability from a traumatic injury, up to 45 calendar days.
(b) employer may not require leave unless 10.200(c), 10.220 or 10.222 apply. (c) Postal Service: no COP for the first 3 days.
10.205 (a) eligibility: (1) traumatic injury causing disability and/or lost time for medical examination and treatment;
(2) CA-1 within 30 days of the date of injury; (3) begin losing time from work within 45 days of the injury.
10.206 election between leave and COP, changeable; (c) leave may not delay or extend the 45-day COP period; any leave used during
the period of eligibility counts towards the 45-day maximum.
10.207 return to work then stops again: remaining COP days paid where (a) CA-2a and elects regular pay; (b) original claim not
denied; (c) the disability recurs and the employee stops work within 45 days of the time the employee first returned to work
following the initial period of disability; (d) pay not continued for the entire 45 days.
10.215 COP maximum 45 calendar days; every day used counts. (a) time lost on the day or shift of the injury does not count (agency
keeps the employee in pay status); (b) the first COP day is the first day disability begins following the date of injury
(within the 45 days following the date of injury), except where the injury occurs before the beginning of the work day or
shift, in which case the date of injury is charged to COP; (c) any part of a day or shift (except the day of injury) counts as a
full day; (d) regular days off are included if COP has been used on the regular work days immediately preceding or following
the regular day(s) off, and medical evidence supports disability; (e) leave used during a period when COP is otherwise payable
counts toward the 45 days as if in COP status; (f) part-time or intermittent schedules: all calendar days on which medical
evidence indicates disability are counted as COP days, regardless of whether the employee was or would have been scheduled.
10.216 COP pay rate (weekly pay; formulas for full-time, part-time, intermittent).
10.217 different job with no loss in pay: COP not chargeable; any actual reduction of pay from the injury: COP paid and counted.
10.220 COP not required when: (a) not a traumatic injury; (b) not a US/Canada citizen; (c) no written claim within 30 days from
the date of injury; (d) injury reported after employment terminated; (e) off premises and not in performance of duty;
(f) willful misconduct / intoxication; (g) work did not stop until more than 45 days following the injury.
10.222 COP may be stopped: (1) no medical evidence within 10 days (reinstated retroactively when provided); (2) not disabled;
(3) refuses suitable alternative position; (4) returns to work with no loss of pay; (5) employment expires; (6) OWCP directs;
(7) COP paid for 45 calendar days.
