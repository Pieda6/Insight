## 38 CFR 3.31 (as amended 91 FR 905, Jan. 9, 2026)
Regardless of VA regulations concerning effective dates of awards, and except as provided in paragraph (c), payment of
monetary benefits based on original, supplemental, or increased awards of compensation ... may not be made for any
period prior to the first day of the calendar month following the month in which the award became effective.
(a) Increased award means an award which is increased because of an added dependent, increase in disability or
disability rating, or reduction in income. The term also includes ... awards pursuant to paragraphs 29 and 30 of the
Schedule for Rating Disabilities except as provided in paragraph (c).
(b) Applies to all original, supplemental, or increased awards unless such awards provide only for continuity of
entitlement with no increase in rate of payment.
(c) Specific exclusions: (3) termination of any withholding ... by reason of recoupment; (4) cost-of-living increases;
(5) Temporary total ratings pursuant to paragraph 29 ... when the entire period of hospitalization or treatment,
including any period of post-hospitalization convalescence, commences and terminates within the same calendar month.
In such cases the period of payment shall commence on the first day of the month in which the hospitalization or
treatment began.

## 38 CFR 4.29
Total rating when a service-connected disability has required hospital treatment ... for a period in excess of 21
days. (a) effective the first day of continuous hospitalization and terminated effective the last day of the month of
hospital discharge (regular discharge or release to non-bed care) ... A temporary release approved as part of the
treatment plan is not an absence.
(1) An authorized absence in excess of 4 days which begins during the first 21 days of hospitalization will be
regarded as the equivalent of hospital discharge effective the first day of such authorized absence. An authorized
absence of 4 days or less which results in a total of more than 8 days of authorized absence during the first 21 days
of hospitalization will be regarded as the equivalent of hospital discharge effective the ninth day of authorized
absence.
(2) Following a period of hospitalization in excess of 21 days, an authorized absence in excess of 14 days or a third
consecutive authorized absence of 14 days will be regarded as the equivalent of hospital discharge and will interrupt
hospitalization effective on the last day of the month in which either the authorized absence in excess of 14 days or
the third 14 day period begins, except where there is a finding that convalescence is required as provided by (e) or
(f). The termination of these total ratings will not be subject to 3.105(e).
(b) Admission for a nonservice-connected disability: if hospital treatment for a service-connected disability is
instituted and continued for a period in excess of 21 days, the total rating is granted from the first day of such
treatment.
(d) VA regulations governing effective dates for increased benefits control.
(e) The total hospital rating if convalescence is required may be continued for periods of 1, 2, or 3 months in
addition to the period provided in (a).

## 38 CFR 4.26 (as amended 88 FR 22917, Apr. 14, 2023)
Both arms, both legs or paired skeletal muscles: combine the right and left ratings, add (not combine) 10 percent of
this value, before further combinations; the result is one disability for ordering and further combinations.
Example: 60, 20, 10, 10 (the two 10s bilateral): order 60, 21, 20; 60 and 21 combine to 68; 68 and 20 to 74; 70.
(a) Arms and legs are the upper and lower extremities as a whole; compensable disabilities affecting use of paired
extremities regardless of location or type of impairment.
(b) Four affected extremities: combine the ratings of all 4 extremities in order of severity and add 10 percent of the
combined value once.
(c) Not applicable unless partial disability of compensable degree in each of 2 paired extremities.
(d) Where the combined evaluation is lower than what could be achieved by not including one or more bilateral
disabilities in the bilateral factor calculation, those disabilities are removed from it and combined separately, to
achieve the combined evaluation most favorable to the veteran.
